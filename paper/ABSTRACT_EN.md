# PICEE-PA - English abstract

Public-sector AI is moving from conversational assistance toward systems that can select tools, orchestrate workflows and create operational side effects. This shifts governance from generated content to the legitimacy and controllability of action. PICEE-PA is a vendor-neutral public-administration application-profile proposal for governing AI-initiated actions through **Policy, Identity, Capability, Execution and Evidence**. It does not claim to invent policy-as-code, agent identity, least privilege, human oversight or audit. Instead, it proposes to connect **administrative authorization** (purpose, process, delegation and accountability) to **technical authorization** (identity, capability, policy enforcement and runtime controls) for each significant action. Two proposed interoperable artefacts are introduced: the **Administrative Execution Envelope (AEE)**, a pre-execution contract, and the **Administrative Evidence Bundle (AEB)**, a post-execution evidence package. PICEE-PA further defines L0-L3 action classes, fail-closed semantics, a threat model and a validation roadmap. It is a design proposal to be implemented and tested, not a standard or certification.
